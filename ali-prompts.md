## First prompts

### 1. Claude Code

We're building a proactive agent that makes managing and understanding the healthcare journey of elderly patients and patients with chronic health conditions easier.

The value we add here is having a consent and interpretability engine that gives patients the power to manage who can access their healthcare data. This will reduce the burden on the patient, healthcare professionals, and family members.

- The way we're helping healthcare professionals is that this consent will be synced to the EHR, so you don't need to go through the pain of asking the patient for each consent.
- The way it's helping patients is that this agent will proactively alert family members about upcoming appointments or next actions to be taken.
- The way it's helping family members is that it disseminates the information in an easy way and helps you help your family member.

We have access to this simulation environment: <https://sim.animahealth.com/control/>.

I need you to get a subagent to understand the data and what we have access to. That will help us understand what we can create as tools, APIs, MCPs, etc.

Get another subagent to explore the ADK we're given: <https://github.com/mycontinuum-com/adk>, as we need to build the agent on that.

Then I need you to focus on a web app that demonstrates:

1. A patient changing consent details inside the app through the UI or by talking to the agent.
2. The agent sending a notification to a family member group about an upcoming appointment.
3. A conversation between a daughter and the agent to explain a test result.
