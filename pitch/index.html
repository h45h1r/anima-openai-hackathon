The solution

Kindred turns every result into the right explanation and next action for every trusted person, under the patient's control.

1. Sees it comingReads the whole trend, not one result.
2. Tells her firstNothing reaches the family until she has been told and says yes.
3. Tells the right people the right wayDaughter gets the numbers. Husband gets the lift to arrange. Son gets a yes or no.

The demo

90 seconds, live against Anima.

  • Eleanor's body. Nine systems coloured from her live record in the Body tab, consent-gated per system; the Home boxes say what needs watching. Trend cards live from the prototype
  • Order the LFT in Anima; advance the clock 121 minutes. The result lands as a real state change.
  • Held. Eleanor's phone: a result has arrived and the practice will talk her through it. Family sees nothing.
  • Her yes. Three versions go out: Grace numbers, Idris logistics, Thomas yes/no. A task appears in Anima's activity trail.
  • Grace asks about a result she cannot see. NOT_SHARED, no hint. She asks for access; Eleanor approves from a card; the GP consent view updates live.
  • The morning check reads the 08:30 appointment from the record and posts to the family group: who can give Eleanor a lift?
