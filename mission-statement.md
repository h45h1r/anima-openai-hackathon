we're building a proactive agent that makes managing and understanding healthcare journey of elderly/chronic health patients easier.

the value we add here is having a consent and interpretability engine that gives power to the patients to manage who can access to their healthcare data. this will reduce other burden on the patient, on the healthcare profession as well as family members.

- the way we're helping healthcare professional is this consent will be synced to ehr so you don't need to go through the pain of asking patient for each consent
- the way it's helping patient is that this agent will be proactive to alert family members about upcoming appointments or next actions to be taken
- the way it's helping family members is that it disseminates the information in an easy way and helps you to help your family member
