Messagey messaging

Messagey is the fictional primary-care messaging workspace beside GP Records and Document Inbox. Open Riverside Practice on the neighbourhood map and choose Messagey. It uses the same team world and patient directory as the other products.

Send a patient message​

  1. Select New message and find any synthetic patient by name or ID.
  2. Enter a subject and message, or choose an editable practice template.
  3. Choose SMS or email and whether to allow replies.
  4. Select Queue message.
  5. Open Delivery simulator and choose Deliver or Fail delivery.

Messages never leave the simulation. A queued message does not appear in the patient's app until it is delivered. Failed messages can be retried; the complete delivery history remains attached to the original message.

Reply from home​

Open Messages at the patient's home, or follow the patient Messages link from a practice conversation. Choose the same patient, open a delivered conversation and send a reply. Replies appear in the practice inbox. A completed conversation or one-way message cannot accept replies. The practice can reopen a completed conversation.

Internal practice notes and assignments are not shown in the patient app. The patient selector lets hackathon teams role-play different synthetic people inside their own world.

Manage the inbox​

Conversations can be assigned to a named clinician or team, supplemented with internal notes and marked done. Done holds completed conversations and offers a reopen action. Patient messages retain their original text and author when colleagues add subsequent messages.

Templates supports creating, editing and archiving reusable SMS and email text. Applying a template copies its content into an editable composer; changing a template does not rewrite previously sent messages.

API​

Read practice conversations and templates with GET /api/sites/gp/messaging-workspace. Read one patient's delivered conversations with GET /api/sites/patient/messaging-workspace?patientId=SIM-000001. Both use the existing team key with GP scope.

Post to /api/sites/gp/actions with type: "messaging_action" and a messagingCommand. Commands are discriminated by kind:

  • create: subject, body, channel (sms or email), allowReply; also supply top-level patientId.
  • send: body, channel.
  • reply: body; the patient route is /api/sites/patient/actions and requires the matching top-level patientId.
  • note: body.
  • assign: assignee; an empty value clears the assignment.
  • complete and reopen.
  • delivery: entryId, status (delivered or failed).
  • retry: entryId.
  • save_template: title, body, channel.
  • archive_template.

Existing resources require top-level resourceId and expectedVersion. Supply an Idempotency-Key header for safely retried requests. A stale version returns a conflict instead of overwriting another participant's changes. Patient messaging commands only accept reply; patient reads and responses omit internal notes and undelivered outgoing messages.
