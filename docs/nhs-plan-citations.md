Source: *Fit for the Future: The 10 Year Health Plan for England* (CP 1350, DHSC, July 2025, corrected 15 July 2025).
Page numbers below are the **printed page numbers** shown in the document footer (PDF page = printed page + 2). Chapter ranges: Exec Summary pp7-16, Ch1 pp17-24, Ch2 pp25-44, Ch3 pp45-56, Ch4 pp57-74, Ch5 pp75-84, Ch6 pp85-94, Ch7 pp95-110, Ch8 pp111-128, Ch9 pp129-140.

All quotes were extracted verbatim from the PDF text layer (`pdftotext`) and checked against the surrounding paragraph. Hyphenation/quote marks follow the source.

## Read this first: what the plan does and does not say

- The plan **does** commit to: NHS App as the full front door by 2028; a Single Patient Record with patient control and a legal duty to make information available to the patient; a `My Health` tool that "will connect to the results of recent tests and investigations"; `My Carer` proxy access for unpaid carers; `My Companion` for explaining conditions/procedures; 95% of people with complex needs having a care plan by 2027; AI to reduce GP admin; universal PROMs/PREMs by 2029; and a new transparency/quality agenda in Chapter 6.
- The plan **does not** contain any sentence about abnormal test results going uncommunicated, missed results, or result-acknowledgement safety nets. HSSIB is mentioned only in the context of rationalising the safety regulator landscape (pp93-94). If the pitch cites the HSIB investigation into "failures in communication or follow-up of abnormal test results" (2019), that must be sourced separately; it is **not** in this document and was not verified here.
- The strongest framing for judges is therefore: CareCircle operationalises three named plan priorities (NHS App front door / SPR patient control / carer proxy access) and closes the gap the plan leaves open (nothing in the App roadmap guarantees a result is *acted on and understood*, only that it is *visible*).
